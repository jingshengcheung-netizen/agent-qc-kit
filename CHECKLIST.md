# Pre-publish QC checklist

Use before shipping agent-assisted research or content. Fail any **HARD** item = do not publish.

## Identity & claims

- [ ] **HARD** Does not imply CFA charterholder / licensed adviser unless true and verified
- [ ] **HARD** No target price, buy/sell recommendation, or return promise for securities
- [ ] Soft plugs (products) are clearly optional and not the main claim

## Facts & sources

- [ ] **HARD** Every material number/quote has a public source URL (and date when known)
- [ ] **HARD** No invented stats or fake "Source:" attributions
- [ ] Unverified items are labeled `unverified` — not silently filled in

## Structure & readability

- [ ] **HARD** First sentence is a complete judgment (not truncated mid-word)
- [ ] One core idea per short post / section
- [ ] No broken quotation marks or cut-off Chinese/English glyphs from length limits

## Agent-specific

- [ ] Tool permissions / citations were not invented by the model
- [ ] If the model refused or failed, the draft does not pretend success
- [ ] Links open and match the claim (spot-check at least the primary source)
